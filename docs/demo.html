                Privacy & Data

                Exactly what leaves your device, and exactly what does not.

                What is collected

                • Counts of keystrokes, grouped only as printable, backspace/delete, and undo.
                • The number of characters in a paste — never the pasted text.
                • Active time per session, the site's domain, and the page path.
                • A histogram of pauses between keystrokes, as bucket counts.
                • How many prompts were submitted on an AI assistant site.
                • Tab-switches away during a graded assessment.

                What is never collected

                • The text you type. Not a word of it, ever.
                • Clipboard contents. Pastes are measured with .length and the string is discarded in the same instant it is read.
                • Ordered keystroke sequences. Rhythm is stored as bucket counts precisely because the ordering is what could leak content — destroying it is the whole safety argument.
                • Page content, screenshots, form values, or your browsing history.
                • Hardware fingerprints. Your device id is a random UUID generated at install, so it can be revoked and does not follow you between accounts.

                What stays on this device

                • Your profile photo — it is stored in this browser and never uploaded.
                • The extension's cached settings copy and its retry queue.

                    How the score works, and what it cannot tell you

                    Your independence score compares your recent sessions against your own earlier sessions — never against other students, and never against a population average. That is a deliberate limit: this project has no student cohort behind it, so any claim about real-world accuracy, F1, AUC, or whether using it causes better outcomes would be invented. It is not measured, so it is not claimed.

                    What the score can honestly say is narrow and useful: how much of your recent tracked work you produced yourself versus pasted in, and whether that share is moving up or down relative to how you were working before. A personal baseline needs a handful of sessions before it means anything, and until then the dashboard says so rather than showing a confident number built on one data point.

                    Any simulated or trial figures used during development are labelled as simulated wherever they appear. They are not shown on this page.
